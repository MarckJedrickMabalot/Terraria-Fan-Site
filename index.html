ZENITH

The most powerful weapon in Terraria. Crafted from 14 legendary swords, it fires a constant swarm of homing, wall-piercing blades. Against Moon Lord it is simply unmatched.

WEAPON · MAGIC GUN

Dropped by Moon Lord. Fires 6 rainbow lasers that converge into one devastating beam after 2 seconds of channeling. The highest single-target DPS of any magic weapon.

NEBULA ARMOR
ARMOR · ENDGAME

Crafted from Nebula Fragments. Killing enemies spawns orbs granting stacking damage, life regen, and mana regen bonuses. At maximum stacks the mage becomes lethal.

BOSS BESTIARY

FROM SLIME TO MOON LORD

Every boss in Terraria, ordered easiest to hardest — complete with idle animations, strategies, and recommended gear for each encounter.

⚔ PRE-HARDMODE
#01 · KING SLIME
PRE-HARDMODE · OPTIONAL

KING SLIME

HP
2,000

The first true boss most players encounter. A giant crowned slime that teleports to close distance and periodically spawns smaller Blue Slimes. As you deal damage, it shrinks — but the teleports become more frequent and erratic.

⚡ STRATEGY

Build a long flat arena and keep moving in one direction. The teleports are predictable — he always lands near you. Use ranged weapons to deal consistent damage from a distance. His spawn rate for smaller slimes increases as HP drops, so burst him down quickly in the final phase.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Boomstick
Hermes Boots
Gold Bow
Gold Armor
Shurikens
#02 · EYE OF CTHULHU
PRE-HARDMODE · EARLY GAME

EYE OF CTHULHU

HP
2,800

A massive flying eyeball — Cthulhu's severed eye — that charges at you in a spiral pattern. At 50% HP it transforms into a more aggressive second phase, revealing a toothed maw and spawning Servants of Cthulhu. This boss teaches players the core lesson of Terraria combat: keep moving.

⚡ STRATEGY

Fight at night on a long horizontal platform. Circle the boss by walking backwards while firing — its spiral dashes are predictable. In phase 2 it becomes faster and spawns minions; focus the main eye and ignore adds. A yoyo or bow weapon works excellently here.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Tungsten Bow
Jester Arrows
Hermes Boots
Gold Armor
Mana Flower
#03 · EATER OF WORLDS
PRE-HARDMODE · CORRUPTION

EATER OF WORLDS

HP
7,500+

A massive worm boss made of connected segments — each with its own HP pool. When segments are destroyed the worm splits into two smaller worms. Killing it yields Shadow Scales for crafting Shadow armor, essential mid-game gear. Crimson worlds get the Brain of Cthulhu instead.

⚡ STRATEGY

Fight in the Corruption using Grenades or a piercing weapon like the Vilethorn — hitting multiple segments at once is key. Don't destroy too many individual segments or you'll spawn multiple smaller worms. Aim to kill full worm sections, not split them endlessly. Platforms over the chasm work perfectly.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Vilethorn
Grenades
Platinum Armor
Hermes Boots
Regen Band
#04 · QUEEN BEE
PRE-HARDMODE · OPTIONAL

QUEEN BEE

HP
3,400

Found in the Underground Jungle, the Queen Bee charges, hovers and fires stingers while summoning bees. She's one of the most rewarding optional pre-HM bosses — dropping the Bee's Knees, Bee Gun, and the incredibly useful Honey Comb accessory.

⚡ STRATEGY

Create a wide arena inside or just outside the Jungle. She alternates between charge attacks and stinger volleys — learn the pattern and dodge accordingly. The Bee Gun she drops is a great weapon for fighting Skeletron. Her honey debuff slows you, so dodge the stingers first. Meteor Armor with Space Gun works beautifully here.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Space Gun
Meteor Armor
Band of Starpower
Hermes Boots
Minishark
#05 · SKELETRON
PRE-HARDMODE · DUNGEON GATE

SKELETRON

HP
4,400

Guardian of the Dungeon. Defeat Skeletron to access the Dungeon, one of the most loot-rich areas in the game. He consists of a spinning head and two hands. Must be defeated before sunrise — if dawn breaks he instantly kills you with a single hit.

⚡ STRATEGY

Destroy both hands first — they deal significant damage and block shots aimed at his head. Once both hands are gone, focus the head. His spin attack is his most dangerous move: use wings or a grappling hook to avoid it. Prioritize movement speed accessories. If you're running low on time, disengage and wait for the next night.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Bee's Knees
Shadow Armor
Spectre Boots
Mana Flower
Regen Band
#06 · WALL OF FLESH
PRE-HARDMODE · FINAL BOSS

WALL OF FLESH

HP
8,000

The final pre-Hardmode boss and the gatekeeper to an entirely new game. The Wall of Flesh spans the entire height of the Underworld and pushes you toward the edge of the map. Destroying it permanently triggers Hardmode — spreading Corruption and Hallow across your world.

⚡ STRATEGY

Build a long platform track spanning the entire Underworld — minimum 2000 blocks. Use Minecart or Wings to keep ahead of the wall. Attack both the Eyes and the Mouth. The Hungries (tentacle minions) get more numerous as HP drops. Prioritize movement and burst the Eyes down fast. Hellfire Arrows and a Molten Fury bow melt this boss.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Molten Fury
Hellfire Arrows
Molten Armor
Obsidian Shield
Minecart
🔥 HARDMODE
#07 · THE DESTROYER
HARDMODE · MECHANICAL

THE DESTROYER

HP
80,000

A mechanical Eater of Worlds made of steel segments that shoot lasers. Unlike the Eater of Worlds, killing individual segments does NOT split it — the whole worm must be destroyed. Each segment fires Probes that spawn and attack independently, making the arena quickly overwhelming.

⚡ STRATEGY

Use piercing weapons — the Dao of Pow, Star Cannon, or a Crystal Serpent to hit multiple segments per shot. Build a high skybridge and use wings to stay above him. Kill Probes fast to prevent laser spam. Daedalus Stormbow with Holy Arrows is one of the strongest options here — it fires arrows straight down through the entire boss body.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Daedalus Stormbow
Holy Arrows
Hallowed Armor
Wings
Megashark
#08 · THE TWINS
HARDMODE · MECHANICAL

THE TWINS

HP
43,000 combined

Two mechanical eyes — Retinazer (fires lasers) and Spazmatism (spits cursed fire and charges). They each have two phases. If one eye is killed while the other remains, the surviving eye enters a supercharged second phase with dramatically increased aggression. The hardest mechanical boss.

⚡ STRATEGY

Reduce both eyes to near-death simultaneously before killing either, or the survivor goes berserk. Spazmatism is more dangerous — kill it slightly before Retinazer. Use high-mobility accessories and a Nimbus Rod to deal passive damage. An arena with multiple rope layers helps with vertical movement. The Death Sickle or Daedalus Stormbow with Holy Arrows are top picks.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Death Sickle
Stormbow
Hallowed Armor
Cross Necklace
Nimbus Rod
#09 · SKELETRON PRIME
HARDMODE · MECHANICAL

SKELETRON PRIME

HP
28,000

The mechanical counterpart to Skeletron. Sports a laser gun, vice, saw, and cannon arm — four deadly appendages that can overwhelm you if left alive. Like its predecessor, it kills you instantly if it survives until dawn. Defeating all three mechanical bosses triggers Plantera.

⚡ STRATEGY

Destroy the arms one by one — prioritize the laser gun arm first as it deals the most consistent damage. Once all arms are down, the head becomes significantly more vulnerable. The head spins rapidly when attacked — create a large open arena for horizontal movement. Ichor arrows or Ichor Flask debuff dramatically boost your damage output here.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Megashark
Crystal Bullets
Titanium Armor
Lightning Boots
Ichor Arrows
#10 · PLANTERA
HARDMODE · JUNGLE GUARDIAN

PLANTERA

HP
30,000

The true hardmode difficulty spike. Plantera must be fought in the Underground Jungle and pulls itself toward you on vine hooks. In phase 2 it sheds its outer petals to reveal a carnivorous core, gaining dramatically increased speed and firing a barrage of seeds, spores, and Plantera's Tentacles.

⚡ STRATEGY

Dig a large circular arena (minimum 80x80 tiles) in the Jungle. Move in large circles — Plantera can only move as fast as her hooks allow. In phase 2 keep circling and avoid the tentacle spawns. Chlorophyte Bullets with a Megashark auto-aim at her bulbs. The Scourge of the Corruptor or Crystal Vile Shard work well for phase 2 especially.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Megashark
Chlorophyte Bullets
Titanium Armor
Uzi
Piranha Gun
#11 · GOLEM
HARDMODE · TEMPLE

GOLEM

HP
9,000 head

A stone colossus fought in the Lihzahrd Temple after defeating Plantera. Known for being one of the easiest hardmode bosses — mostly because by the time you reach it you're massively overpowered. Fires laser beams, summons fists, and has a detachable head phase.

⚡ STRATEGY

Destroy the fists first, then focus the head. In phase 2 the head detaches and flies freely — it's significantly faster, so keep moving. The temple floor provides convenient solid ground. Almost any endgame weapon melts Golem quickly. Stardust Dragon or Twins-era equipment trivializes this fight completely.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Terra Blade
Chlorophyte Armor
Destroyer Emblem
Stardust Dragon
Celestial Shell
#12 · LUNATIC CULTIST
HARDMODE · LUNAR GATEWAY

LUNATIC CULTIST

HP
32,000

The gateway to the Lunar Events and endgame. A sorcerer with four elemental dragons that it can summon. One key mechanic: if you attack a Cultist Clone (instead of the real one) during the ritual phase, the Lunatic Cultist regains health and resets. Identifying the real cultist is the challenge.

⚡ STRATEGY

During the clone ritual, wait for all cultists to appear and then attack the one that is NOT casting a symbol. The real Lunatic Cultist is always the one offset from the group during the ritual. During combat, dodge all projectiles aggressively — he summons Ancient Ice, Lightning, and Fire projectiles in rotation. High DPS weapons end the fight before patterns get overwhelming.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Razorblade Typhoon
Spectre Armor
Celestial Shell
Sniper Rifle
Leaf Blower
#13 · MOON LORD
HARDMODE · FINAL BOSS

MOON LORD

HP
145,000 total

The supreme boss of Terraria — Cthulhu's brother, with 145,000 total HP spread across two hand eyes, a forehead eye, and a core. His True Eye of Cthulhu attack fires an unavoidable Moon Bite debuff that blocks life regen. Each of his eyes must be destroyed before the core is exposed. Drops all four endgame fragment weapons.

⚡ STRATEGY

Build a massive skybridge with campfires, heart lanterns, and a nurse for emergency healing. Prioritize one hand at a time, then the forehead eye — destroying them exposes the core. Use the Nurse to heal during the True Eye phase. The Zenith, Last Prism, Phantasm with Holy Arrows, and Stardust Dragon all obliterate this boss. Dodge the Phantasmal Deathray by moving vertically — it only tracks horizontally.

RECOMMENDED GEAR

Phantasm
Holy Arrows
Solar Flare
Stardust Dragon
Last Prism
Zenith
